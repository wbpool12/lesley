About Lesley

A teacher gone realtor with a passion for helping families find home.

👩‍🏫

From the Classroom to Real Estate

Lesley is a teacher gone realtor, having spent almost 15 years in education before deciding it was time for a new challenge. She didn't go into real estate due to some a-ha moment — change happens as a result of influences in life and the decision to take action.

She grew up in South Arlington and attended a small private school from grades K–12, graduating as Valedictorian of her high school class before attending Texas Tech University. She graduated from Tech with a degree in Early Childhood Education and taught in Grand Prairie ISD, Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, and also taught for 2 years in Georgia.

She left the classroom because she believes the system is doing children a disservice — that politics and money drive education decisions.

Life Outside of Real Estate

Lesley has been divorced and is now remarried to her wonderful husband, Blake. She has a 5½ year old son with her ex-husband, and she thinks that's important to know about someone, because divorce and children change lives.

They currently live in Keller and she loves the community — friendly and accepting people who have hearts for sharing and caring.

Most of her family lives in California — she has two sisters who left Texas after graduating college, and they try to visit California a couple of times a year.
