About Lesley Pool

A teacher turned realtor — service is at the heart of everything I do.

Why Work With Me

Before real estate, I spent nearly 15 years in education—teaching across multiple districts and states. Teaching shaped who I am. It taught me how to listen, advocate, communicate clearly, and show up consistently for people who trust me.

Making a major career shift in my mid-30s wasn’t something I took lightly. That same passion, preparation, and dedication I brought to the classroom is exactly what I bring into real estate—only now, I help people make one of the most important decisions of their lives.

My approach is rooted in health and wellness—not just physically, but mentally, emotionally, and financially. Buying or selling a home can be stressful, and I believe the process should feel supported, informed, and aligned—not rushed or transactional.

My unique skill sets are intention, integrity, and communication. Every recommendation, strategy, and conversation comes back to what serves you best.

I can’t promise a perfectly smooth transaction every time. But I can promise this: I will advocate for you relentlessly, communicate clearly, and always operate with your goals—and your well-being—at the center of every decision.

My Story

At my core, I’m a family-first person who believes that a good life is built intentionally—through meaningful relationships, healthy habits, and work that truly serves others.

I graduated from Texas Tech University with a degree in Early Childhood Education and spent 15 years in public education. While that chapter shaped me deeply, today I’m focused on building a life and career that align with my values, my family, and my overall well-being.

I’m married to my husband, Blake, and together we’re raising our two sweet boys who are seven and 9 months. Life has taken me through seasons of change—including divorce, remarriage and a journey with infertility—and those experiences have given me a deep appreciation for resilience, growth, and compassion. Family dynamics matter, life isn’t always linear, and I believe understanding that about someone is important.

We live in Keller, a community I genuinely love for its welcoming spirit, kind people, and strong sense of connection. Most of my extended family lives in California, including my two sisters—my best friends—so we soak up time together whenever we can.

Health and wellness are a huge part of my life—not in a perfectionist way, but in a sustainable, intentional one. I’m passionate about taking care of my body and mind so I can show up fully for the people I love and the work I do. You’ll usually find me prioritizing movement, sunshine, long walks, yoga, and learning how to live better—not just busier.

I love a challenge, I love helping people, and I believe kindness still matters. One of my personal goals—every single day—is to leave people feeling seen, supported, and better than before our paths crossed. That philosophy guides how I live, how I work, and how I show up.

Best always,
Lesley Pool
