- How It Works -

The Buying Process

1

Determine your budget and get pre-approved

Before starting the home buying process, assess your finances and establish a realistic budget. Getting pre-approved for a mortgage will help you understand your borrowing capacity and provide confidence to sellers that you are a serious buyer.

2

Find a trusted real estate agent

Partnering with a knowledgeable and reliable real estate agent is invaluable when buying a home. They will understand your preferences, search for suitable properties, schedule viewings, and guide you through the entire process, from making offers to negotiating terms.

3

Search for your ideal home

With the assistance of your agent, begin the search for your dream home. Discuss your requirements and preferences, including location, size, amenities, and price range. Your agent will provide you with access to comprehensive listing databases and help you narrow down the options that best match your criteria.

4

Make an offer and negotiate

When you find a home that meets your needs, your agent will assist you in making an offer. They will provide guidance on pricing, contingencies, and other terms to ensure a strong and competitive offer. If necessary, negotiations may take place with the seller to reach mutually agreeable terms.

5

Complete due diligence and close the deal

Once your offer is accepted, you will enter the due diligence phase. This typically includes inspections, obtaining a mortgage, reviewing property disclosures, and finalizing any contingencies. Your agent will coordinate these processes and ensure that everything progresses smoothly. Finally, at the closing, all necessary paperwork is signed, funds are transferred, and the keys are handed over, officially making you the proud owner of your new home.
