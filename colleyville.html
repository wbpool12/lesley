Small Town Living with Big City Life!

Colleyville, Texas

Welcome to Colleyville

Tucked between Dallas and Fort Worth in Tarrant County, Colleyville, TX is a wealthy suburban city of approximately 27,000 residents known for its rural charm, tree-lined streets, and spacious lots. Once a quiet farming community, Colleyville has grown into one of the most desirable neighborhoods in the DFW metroplex while retaining its peaceful, small-town character. Residents enjoy upscale shopping and dining at the Village at Colleyville, excellent schools through both Grapevine-Colleyville ISD and Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD, and a strong sense of community. With large estate-style properties, winding roads shaded by mature oaks, and a commitment to preserving open space, Colleyville offers an exceptional quality of life for families and professionals seeking a refined yet relaxed Texas lifestyle.

Location of Colleyville, TX

Colleyville is centrally located in the heart of the DFW metroplex, bordered by Grapevine to the north, Southlake to the northwest, Bedford to the south, Euless to the southeast, and Hurst to the southwest. The city sits along the SH 26 and Glade Road corridors, providing convenient access to surrounding communities. Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport is just minutes away, making Colleyville an ideal home base for frequent travelers and business professionals. Despite its proximity to major urban centers, Colleyville maintains a distinctly suburban and semi-rural atmosphere with winding residential streets, generous lot sizes, and abundant green space throughout its neighborhoods.

5 Reasons You'll Love It

  1. Large Lots and Acreage

    Colleyville is known for its generous lot sizes, with many properties sitting on one acre or more. Enjoy the privacy, space, and rural feel that is increasingly rare in the DFW metroplex, with room for pools, gardens, and outdoor entertaining.

  2. Top-Rated Schools

    Colleyville is served by the highly acclaimed Grapevine-Colleyville ISD, home to Colleyville Heritage High School. Families benefit from excellent academics, competitive athletics, and a wide range of extracurricular programs that consistently earn top marks statewide.

  3. Safe, Low-Crime Community

    Colleyville consistently ranks as one of the safest cities in Texas. Its quiet, residential neighborhoods and strong community engagement create an environment where families feel secure and neighbors look out for one another.

  4. No Commercial Clutter

    Unlike many suburban cities, Colleyville has maintained strict zoning that limits heavy commercial development. The result is a peaceful, semi-rural atmosphere with tree-canopied roads and minimal strip malls or big-box retail.

  5. Proximity to DFW Airport

    Living in Colleyville means you are just minutes from one of the busiest airports in the world. This makes travel a breeze for business professionals and families who fly frequently.

5 Reasons You May Not

  1. Higher Property Taxes

    Premium home values and multiple taxing entities mean property tax bills in Colleyville can be substantial compared to other parts of the metroplex.

  2. Limited Nightlife

    Colleyville is primarily residential. For nightlife, entertainment districts, and late-night dining, residents typically head to nearby Grapevine, Southlake, or Fort Worth.

  3. HOA Restrictions

    Many Colleyville neighborhoods have homeowners associations with detailed guidelines on exterior modifications, landscaping, and property use that some buyers may find restrictive.

  4. Distance from Downtown Dallas

    While close to DFW Airport, Colleyville is roughly 30 miles from downtown Dallas, which can mean a lengthy commute during peak traffic hours.

  5. Limited Public Transit

    There is minimal public transportation serving Colleyville. Residents rely almost entirely on personal vehicles for daily commuting and errands.

Home Styles In Colleyville, TX

Colleyville is renowned for its impressive custom-built homes set on expansive lots. The city's residential landscape features a mix of stately traditional estates, elegant French country designs, and sleek contemporary custom builds. With many properties exceeding one acre, homeowners enjoy generous setbacks, mature tree coverage, and resort-style outdoor living spaces that define the Colleyville lifestyle.

Traditional / Estate

The hallmark of Colleyville real estate, traditional estate homes feature brick and stone exteriors, grand entryways, and expansive floor plans on lots of one acre or more. These properties often include circular driveways, manicured landscaping, and luxurious outdoor living areas with pools and covered patios.

French Country

French country-style homes are a popular choice in Colleyville, characterized by steep pitched rooflines, stone and stucco exteriors, arched doorways, and copper accents. These elegant residences blend Old World charm with modern amenities, often featuring chef's kitchens, wine cellars, and beautifully landscaped grounds.

Custom Contemporary

A growing trend in Colleyville, custom contemporary homes feature clean lines, open-concept layouts, walls of windows, and high-end finishes. These newer builds incorporate smart home technology, energy-efficient systems, and seamless indoor-outdoor transitions that appeal to modern buyers seeking luxury with a fresh aesthetic.

Schools

Colleyville is primarily served by two outstanding school districts. Grapevine-Colleyville ISD (GCISD) covers the majority of the city and is home to Colleyville Heritage High School, widely recognized for academic excellence and competitive athletics. Portions of southern Colleyville fall within Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD (HEB ISD), another well-regarded district. Both districts offer strong elementary, middle, and high school programs, making Colleyville one of the most sought-after communities for families prioritizing education in the DFW metroplex.
