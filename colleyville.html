Colleyville, TX

About Colleyville

Colleyville is an affluent, tree-lined community nestled in the heart of the DFW metroplex. Known for its estate-style homes, generous lot sizes, and a peaceful, semi-rural atmosphere, Colleyville offers a distinguished living experience that attracts families and professionals seeking both elegance and tranquility.

The city is served by the highly regarded Grapevine-Colleyville ISD (GCISD), making it a top choice for families who prioritize exceptional education. With close proximity to DFW International Airport, residents enjoy the convenience of quick travel access without sacrificing the quiet, residential character of the community.

Colleyville's picturesque streetscapes, mature trees, and spacious properties create a neighborhood feel that is hard to find elsewhere in the metroplex. The city's commitment to preserving green space and maintaining low-density development ensures a lifestyle that feels both exclusive and welcoming.

Why Live in Colleyville?

Estate-Style Homes

Colleyville is known for its spacious properties, custom-built estates, and beautifully landscaped lots that provide privacy, luxury, and room to grow.

Top-Rated GCISD Schools

Grapevine-Colleyville ISD consistently ranks among the best in the state, offering strong academics, competitive athletics, and robust extracurricular programs.

Tree-Lined Streets

Mature trees, winding roads, and generous setbacks give Colleyville its signature charm and a peaceful, park-like atmosphere throughout the city.

Close to DFW Airport

Just minutes from DFW International Airport, Colleyville is ideal for frequent travelers and professionals who need convenient air travel access.

Dining & Shopping

Colleyville Boulevard and surrounding areas offer a curated selection of upscale dining, boutique shopping, and local favorites.

Community & Safety

Consistently ranked among the safest cities in Texas, Colleyville offers a tight-knit community with local events, farmers markets, and a strong neighborhood spirit.
