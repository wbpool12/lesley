Welcome to Colleyville

Nestled in the heart of the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, Grapevine, TX is a captivating city that seamlessly blends history, charm, and vibrant modernity. Known as the "Christmas Capital of Texas," Grapevine delights visitors with its enchanting atmosphere, year-round festivities, and warm hospitality. Steeped in rich heritage, this picturesque city offers a glimpse into its past through its beautifully preserved historic downtown, where quaint shops, cozy eateries, and art galleries line the streets. Beyond its historic allure, Grapevine boasts a thriving wine culture, boasting a multitude of wineries and vineyards that invite visitors to savor the region's finest wines. From its scenic lakeside trails to its world-class shopping and dining options at Grapevine Mills Mall and the Gaylord Texan Resort, Grapevine provides a perfect blend of small-town charm and contemporary attractions, making it an exceptional destination for both locals and tourists alike.

Location of Colleyville, TX

Located in the northeastern part of the state of Texas, Grapevine is nestled within the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. It is situated northwest of Dallas and northeast of Fort Worth, making it conveniently accessible for both local residents and travelers. Grapevine is positioned near the shores of Grapevine Lake, a picturesque reservoir that offers opportunities for water recreation and outdoor activities. Its prime location provides easy access to major transportation hubs, including Dallas/Fort Worth International Airport, which is just minutes away. Surrounded by the rolling hills and scenic landscapes of North Texas, Grapevine enjoys a mild climate, making it an appealing destination year-round. Whether you're seeking the amenities of a bustling metropolitan area or the tranquility of natural beauty, Grapevine's location offers the best of both worlds.

Home Styles In Colleyville, TX

Colleyville, TX embraces a captivating array of architectural styles, embodying the city's rich history and dynamic character. From charming Victorian and Craftsman-style homes that evoke nostalgia with intricate details and ornate woodwork, to contemporary and ranch-style residences that feature open floor plans and seamless indoor-outdoor living, Grapevine offers something for every taste. Mediterranean and Spanish-inspired architecture add a touch of allure with stucco exteriors, tiled roofs, and elegant arched entryways.

Victorian

The Victorian-style homes in Grapevine exhibit exquisite craftsmanship, intricate detailing, and ornate features. These houses often boast steep roofs, decorative trim, colorful exteriors, and wraparound porches.

Craftsman

Craftsman-style homes are highly sought after in Grapevine. They typically feature low-pitched roofs, wide front porches, and prominent gables. These homes emphasize natural materials, such as wood siding and stone accents.

Mediterranean

Mediterranean-style homes have gained popularity in Grapevine, offering a touch of elegance and sophistication. These houses often showcase stucco exteriors, terracotta roofs, arched windows, and wrought-iron accents.

Schools

Grapevine-Colleyville Independent School District is a public school district based in Grapevine, Texas, USA. The district serves most of the cities of Grapevine and Colleyville and includes small portions of Euless, Hurst, and Southlake.

Places of Interest

Colleyville is a city in northeastern Tarrant County, Texas, United States, centrally located in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. A wealthy suburb of the Dallas/Fort Worth area, Colleyville was originally a small farm town in the 19th century. The population was 22,807 at the 2010 census
