Grapevine, TX

About Grapevine

Grapevine is a charming city rich in history, culture, and small-town warmth. Proudly known as the Christmas Capital of Texas, this beloved community captivates residents and visitors alike with its historic Main Street, thriving wine trails, and eclectic mix of shops and restaurants.

Nestled along the shores of Grapevine Lake, the city offers scenic trails, waterfront recreation, and a relaxed lifestyle that feels worlds away from the bustle of the metroplex. From Grapevine Mills to the iconic Gaylord Texan Resort, there is always something to explore.

Families choose Grapevine for its excellent Grapevine-Colleyville ISD (GCISD) schools, safe and charming neighborhoods, and year-round festivals and community events that bring people together.

Architectural Styles

Grapevine's residential landscape reflects its heritage and evolving character.

♣

Victorian

Exquisite craftsmanship, ornate detailing, steep roofs, decorative trim, and wraparound porches capture the elegance of a bygone era in Grapevine's historic core.

♚

Craftsman

Low-pitched roofs with wide eaves, generous front porches, tapered columns, prominent gables, and natural materials define this beloved style throughout the city.

⛪

Traditional Ranch

Single-story living with open layouts, attached garages, brick exteriors, and spacious lots that reflect the relaxed Texas lifestyle Grapevine is known for.

Why Live in Grapevine?

Historic Main Street

A walkable downtown featuring local boutiques, wine tasting rooms, art galleries, and restaurants with a character all its own.

Grapevine Lake

Over 7,000 acres of water and 60 miles of shoreline provide boating, fishing, kayaking, hiking, and lakeside relaxation for the whole family.

Wine Trails & Culture

Grapevine's Urban Wine Trail features award-winning wineries and tasting rooms, making it a unique cultural destination in the DFW metroplex.

Excellent Schools

GCISD is highly regarded for its strong academics, dedicated teachers, and wide range of extracurricular opportunities for students of all ages.

Year-Round Festivals

From GrapeFest to the Christmas Capital of Texas celebration, the city hosts dozens of festivals and events that foster a strong community spirit.

Central Location

Minutes from DFW Airport with easy access to Dallas and Fort Worth, Grapevine offers the perfect blend of small-town charm and big-city convenience.
