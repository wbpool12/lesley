Grapevine, TX

A charming city that blends rich history, small-town warmth, and vibrant modernity in the heart of DFW.

Welcome to Grapevine

Proudly known as the Christmas Capital of Texas, Grapevine captivates residents and visitors with its historic downtown, thriving wine culture, and eclectic attractions.

From Grapevine Mills to the Gaylord Texan Resort and scenic lakeside trails, there's no shortage of things to do. Families enjoy excellent schools, safe neighborhoods, and year-round festivals and community events.

Architectural Styles

Grapevine's residential landscape reflects its heritage and evolving character.

♣

Victorian

Exquisite craftsmanship, ornate detailing, steep roofs, decorative trim, and wraparound porches capture the elegance of a bygone era.
