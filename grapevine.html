Grapevine, TX

A charming city that seamlessly blends rich history, small-town warmth, and vibrant modernity in the heart of DFW.

Welcome to Grapevine

Proudly known as the Christmas Capital of Texas, Grapevine captivates residents and visitors alike with its enchanting historic downtown, thriving wine culture, and an eclectic mix of attractions that make it one of the most desirable cities in the metroplex.

From the sprawling retail destination of Grapevine Mills to the world-renowned Gaylord Texan Resort, and the scenic lakeside trails along Grapevine Lake, there is no shortage of things to see and do. Families enjoy excellent schools, safe neighborhoods, and a calendar packed with festivals, live music, and community events year-round.

Architectural Styles

Grapevine's residential landscape reflects its storied heritage and evolving character.

♣

Victorian

Showcasing exquisite craftsmanship and ornate detailing, Victorian homes in Grapevine feature steeply pitched roofs, decorative trim work, and inviting wraparound porches. These residences capture the elegance and romance of a bygone era while standing as beloved landmarks throughout the city's historic districts.

⛺

Craftsman

Celebrated for their handcrafted appeal, Craftsman-style homes are distinguished by low-pitched roofs with wide eaves, generous front porches supported by tapered columns, and prominent gabled rooflines. Natural materials and thoughtful proportions give these homes an enduring, welcoming character.
