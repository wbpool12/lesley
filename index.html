About Lesley

Real Estate With Heart

Buying or selling a home is more than a transaction — it’s a life decision. I help North Texas families navigate every step of their real estate journey with clarity, care, and thoughtful strategy.

Before real estate, I spent 15 years in education. That foundation of patience, advocacy, and genuine care shapes everything I do for my clients today. My approach is rooted in wellness, intention, and honest communication. When you work with me, you get more than an agent — you get a partner who truly listens.
