Keller, TX

About Keller

Keller is a family-oriented community that perfectly blends suburban comfort with convenient city access. Located in the heart of the DFW metroplex, this growing city offers a welcoming atmosphere, excellent schools, and a strong sense of neighborhood pride that makes it a wonderful place to call home.

Lesley Pool is proud to call Keller home. Living and working in this community gives her firsthand knowledge of its neighborhoods, schools, parks, and local culture -- insight that helps her clients find the perfect fit.

Families are drawn to Keller for its highly regarded Keller Independent School District, abundant green spaces like Bear Creek Park, and a thriving community calendar filled with events and activities. With a suburban feel and easy access to everything the metroplex has to offer, Keller delivers an outstanding quality of life.

Why Live in Keller?

Top-Rated Keller ISD

Keller Independent School District is one of the largest and most respected districts in North Texas, known for academic excellence, championship athletics, and strong fine arts programs.

Bear Creek Park & Trails

The crown jewel of Keller's park system, Bear Creek Park offers sports fields, playgrounds, a splash pad, fishing pond, and miles of scenic trails for walking and biking.

Family-Friendly Neighborhoods

From established subdivisions to newer master-planned communities, Keller offers a range of homes with spacious lots, tree-lined streets, and a true neighborhood feel.

Growing Community

Keller continues to grow with new dining, shopping, and entertainment options while maintaining its small-town charm and community spirit.

Central DFW Location

Easy access to major highways puts Dallas, Fort Worth, and DFW Airport within a short drive, making Keller ideal for commuters and travelers alike.

Community Events

Keller's events calendar includes Keller Fest, farmers markets, outdoor concerts, and holiday celebrations that bring residents together throughout the year.
