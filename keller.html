Where Community Meets Comfort

Keller, Texas

Welcome to Keller

Nestled northwest of Fort Worth along the Highway 377 corridor, Keller is one of North Texas's most desirable family-friendly suburbs. With a population of approximately 47,000 residents, this thriving city strikes an ideal balance between small-town warmth and big-city accessibility. Keller is anchored by the highly rated Keller Independent School District, which consistently earns top marks for academic excellence, competitive athletics, and robust extracurricular offerings—making it a magnet for families seeking the best educational opportunities in the region.

The heart of the community beats in Old Town Keller, a charming district of locally owned shops, restaurants, and gathering spaces that host community events throughout the year. Nature lovers enjoy Bear Creek Park and its scenic trail system, while the Keller Pointe recreation center provides year-round swimming, fitness, and youth programming. With median home prices ranging from $500,000 to $600,000, Keller offers an attainable entry point into upscale suburban living—delivering spacious homes, excellent schools, and a genuine sense of community that larger neighboring cities often lack.

Conveniently located near Interstate 35W and Highway 377, Keller provides straightforward access to Fort Worth, the Alliance corridor, and the broader DFW metroplex. Whether you are drawn by the top-tier schools, the tree-lined neighborhoods, or the welcoming spirit of a city that still knows its neighbors by name, Keller is a place where families put down roots and stay for generations.

Location of Keller, TX

Keller is situated in northeast Tarrant County, approximately 15 miles northwest of downtown Fort Worth and 35 miles west of downtown Dallas. The city is bordered by Southlake to the east, North Richland Hills to the south, and Roanoke and Westlake to the north. Its central position along Highway 377 and proximity to Interstate 35W make commuting to Fort Worth, the Alliance Town Center employment hub, and DFW International Airport convenient for residents who work across the metroplex.

Living in Keller: The Full Picture

5 Reasons You'll Love It

  • Keller ISD Schools — Consistently rated among the top school districts in Texas, Keller ISD serves over 35,000 students across award-winning campuses with strong academics, athletics, and fine arts programs. Families move to Keller specifically for these schools.
  • Family-Friendly Community — Keller is built for families. From neighborhood block parties and youth sports leagues to the annual Keller Fest celebration, this is a city where children ride bikes on safe streets and parents know their neighbors.
  • Affordable Luxury — With median home prices between $500,000 and $600,000, Keller delivers spacious lots, quality construction, and upscale finishes at a lower price point than neighboring Southlake or Colleyville, offering genuine value for your investment.
  • Bear Creek Park & Trails — Bear Creek Park is the crown jewel of Keller's park system, featuring miles of paved and natural-surface trails winding through mature trees and creek beds. It is the perfect escape for joggers, cyclists, and families seeking outdoor recreation.
  • Old Town Keller Charm — The revitalized Old Town Keller district offers a walkable collection of local boutiques, restaurants, coffee shops, and event venues. Seasonal farmers markets and live music events give the area a vibrant, small-town character.

5 Reasons You May Not

  • Distance from Downtown Dallas — At roughly 35 miles from downtown Dallas, daily commuters heading east should expect 45 to 60 minutes of drive time during peak hours, especially without a direct highway route.
  • Highway 377 Traffic — As Keller's main commercial corridor, Highway 377 experiences heavy congestion during rush hours and weekends. Road widening projects are ongoing, but traffic remains a common frustration for residents.
  • Limited Public Transit — Keller does not have direct access to DART rail or TRE commuter rail. Public transportation options are minimal, making personal vehicles a necessity for nearly all daily activities.
  • Suburban Sprawl — Rapid growth has brought strip malls and chain retail along major corridors. Some long-time residents feel the city is losing its small-town identity as new development continues to expand outward.
  • Summer Heat — Like much of North Texas, Keller endures intense summer heat with temperatures regularly exceeding 100 degrees Fahrenheit from June through August. Outdoor activities often shift to early morning or evening hours during peak summer.

Home Styles In Keller, TX

Keller's housing stock reflects its evolution from a rural ranching community to a polished suburb. You will find everything from sprawling single-story ranches on half-acre lots to newly constructed modern farmhouses in master-planned communities. Most homes feature generous square footage, open floor plans, and three-car garages—built for the way Texas families actually live.

Ranch

The single-story ranch is one of Keller's most popular home styles, prized for its accessibility and spacious, open layouts. These homes typically sit on larger lots and feature wide frontages, attached garages, and covered patios ideal for outdoor entertaining. Many of Keller's established neighborhoods from the 1990s and 2000s are anchored by well-maintained ranch homes with mature landscaping, offering comfortable living without the need to navigate stairs. Updated ranches often include vaulted ceilings, gourmet kitchens, and resort-style backyard pools.

Traditional

Traditional two-story homes dominate Keller's newer subdivisions and established family neighborhoods. Characterized by brick and stone exteriors, symmetrical facades, and pitched rooflines, these homes deliver timeless curb appeal and efficient use of space. Inside, you will typically find formal dining rooms, dedicated study spaces, upstairs game rooms, and four to five bedrooms suited for growing families. Neighborhoods like Hidden Lakes, Bourland Oaks, and Marshall Ridge showcase traditional architecture at its finest, with community amenities including pools, playgrounds, and walking trails.

Modern Farmhouse

The modern farmhouse has become the defining style of Keller's newest construction. Blending rustic charm with contemporary design, these homes feature clean lines, board-and-batten siding, metal roof accents, and expansive front porches. Inside, open-concept living spaces flow into chef-grade kitchens with oversized islands, shiplap accent walls, and large windows that flood rooms with natural light. Modern farmhouses in Keller's latest communities often include smart-home technology, energy-efficient systems, and flexible bonus spaces that adapt to work-from-home lifestyles.

Schools in Keller, TX

Keller Independent School District

Keller ISD is the backbone of this community and one of the primary reasons families choose to call Keller home. Serving over 35,000 students across more than 40 campuses, the district is recognized as one of the top-performing school districts in the state of Texas. Keller ISD boasts a graduation rate exceeding 96%, with a strong emphasis on college readiness, career preparation, and well-rounded student development.

Notable campuses include Keller High School, Central High School, and Timber Creek High School, each offering Advanced Placement courses, dual-credit college programs, and nationally competitive athletic and fine arts programs. The district's elementary and middle schools consistently earn high ratings from the Texas Education Agency, and many campuses have received National Blue Ribbon School recognition.

Beyond academics, Keller ISD invests heavily in modern facilities, technology integration, and special programs including STEM academies, gifted and talented education, and comprehensive special education services. The district's commitment to excellence makes it a cornerstone of Keller's identity and a powerful driver of property values throughout the city.

Places of Interest in Keller, TX

Old Town Keller is the cultural and social heart of the city. This revitalized historic district along Olive and Main Streets features a curated mix of locally owned restaurants, boutique shops, coffee houses, and event spaces. Throughout the year, Old Town hosts community gatherings including seasonal farmers markets, live music nights, outdoor movie screenings, and the beloved annual Keller Fest. The area's walkable layout and small-town atmosphere make it a favorite weekend destination for residents and visitors alike.

Bear Creek Park is Keller's premier outdoor destination, spanning over 100 acres of natural beauty along Bear Creek. The park features miles of paved and unpaved trails perfect for hiking, jogging, and mountain biking, along with picnic pavilions, playgrounds, fishing ponds, and open green spaces. The trail system connects to neighboring parks, creating an extensive network for outdoor enthusiasts. Bear Creek Park is a sanctuary for wildlife observation and a peaceful retreat from the pace of suburban life.

The Keller Pointe is the city's flagship recreation center, offering an indoor aquatic center with lap pools, water slides, and a lazy river, a full-service fitness center, gymnasium, indoor running track, and dedicated spaces for group fitness classes. The facility also provides youth programming, summer camps, senior activities, and community meeting rooms. A family membership to Keller Pointe is one of the best values in the city and serves as a social hub for residents of all ages.

Keller Town Center is the city's primary retail and dining corridor along Highway 377, anchored by major retailers, grocery stores, and a diverse selection of restaurants ranging from casual eateries to sit-down dining. The Town Center area also includes medical offices, banking services, and entertainment options, providing residents with convenient access to everyday necessities without leaving the city limits.

Hidden Lakes Golf Course is a beautifully maintained 18-hole public course nestled within one of Keller's most established residential communities. The course offers a challenging yet enjoyable layout with rolling terrain, mature trees, and scenic water features. A fully stocked pro shop, practice facilities, and a clubhouse with dining make it a popular destination for golfers of all skill levels. The surrounding Hidden Lakes neighborhood is one of Keller's most sought-after addresses.
