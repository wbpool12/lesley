For Landlords

Comprehensive services to help you rent out your property with ease and confidence.

✓

Rental Listing & Marketing

Crafting detailed and appealing listings with high-quality photos and accurate descriptions. Utilizing online platforms, real estate websites, and social media to reach a wide audience of qualified prospective tenants.

✓

Tenant Screening

Thorough screening to find reliable and responsible individuals who will take care of your property. The process includes conducting background and credit checks, verifying employment and income, and checking references to help ensure tenants who will treat your property well and pay rent on time.

✓

Lease Agreements

Once suitable tenants are selected, a comprehensive lease agreement is prepared outlining terms and conditions. All legal requirements are met, including security deposit collection and adherence to local rental regulations.

✓

Property Management

Ongoing management of your rental property once the lease is signed, including addressing maintenance issues, collecting rent, and maintaining regular communication with tenants to keep everything running smoothly.

✓

Market Expertise

With a deep understanding of the local rental market, Lesley helps determine the optimal rental price to attract qualified tenants and uses an extensive network and effective marketing strategies to ensure broad exposure for your property.
