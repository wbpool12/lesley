Selling Your Home

Lesley brings a strategic, results-driven approach to every home sale — combining market expertise, professional marketing, and clear communication to help you sell with confidence and maximize your return.

The Selling Process

1

Home Evaluation & Pricing Strategy

I analyze current market conditions, recent comparable sales, and your home's unique features to develop a pricing strategy that attracts buyers and maximizes your return.

2

Preparing Your Home for Market

From staging recommendations to minor repairs, I guide you through the steps that make the biggest impact on how buyers perceive your home.

3

Professional Marketing & Exposure

Your home gets the attention it deserves with professional photography, compelling listings, targeted digital marketing, and broad MLS exposure.

4

Showings, Offers & Negotiation

I manage showings, review every offer carefully, and negotiate skillfully on your behalf to secure the best price and terms.

5

Closing & Transition

I coordinate all closing details and keep you informed at every milestone, ensuring a smooth transition from contract to closing day.

  • Professional photography and marketing that showcases your home at its absolute best
  • Clear, consistent communication so you're never left wondering what's happening
  • Skilled negotiation for maximum value — protecting your bottom line at every turn
  • Smooth, supported closing process with every detail managed and every question answered
