## PAGE 9: TREC CONTRACT / BOUNDARY ISSUES (/texas-real-estate-contract-boundary-issues/)

**Page Title:** Understanding the Real Estate TREC 1-4 Family Residential Contract: What Buyers and Sellers Need to Know - Lesley Pool Real Estate

**Published:** February 26, 2025

**Intro:**
The TREC 1-4 Family Residential Contract (Resale) is the standard document used in Texas to outline the terms of a home buying or selling transaction.

**Section 6A - Boundary-Related Title Policy Options:**

**Option 1:**
The title policy will not cover any potential issues related to boundary disputes, survey conflicts, or encroachments. This offers minimal protection and places more risk on the buyer.

**Option 2:**
The title policy will provide broader coverage, excluding only shortages in area, but covering boundary disputes, encroachments, and protrusions. You can negotiate whether the buyer or seller will pay for the amendment, which is a small fee compared to the potential cost of future disputes.

**Advice:**
If you're unsure which option is best, consult with your real estate agent or title company to ensure you're making an informed decision.
