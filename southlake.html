Southlake, TX

A picturesque city known for upscale neighborhoods, exceptional schools, and strong sense of community.

Discover Southlake Living

Southlake offers an unparalleled quality of life that blends suburban tranquility with cosmopolitan convenience. Nestled amid rolling hills and scenic landscapes, this vibrant city provides residents with a serene setting that feels worlds away from the bustle of everyday life.

Families are drawn to Southlake for its top-rated Carroll Independent School District, beautifully maintained neighborhoods, and an abundance of parks and recreation. With its prime proximity to the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, residents enjoy easy access to world-class dining, entertainment, and major employers — all while coming home to a close-knit, welcoming community.

⛪
★

Southlake Town Square

At the heart of the city lies Southlake Town Square — a premier mixed-use destination that serves as the social and cultural hub of the community. With its charming streetscapes and open-air plazas, Town Square is home to boutique shops offering curated fashion, home décor, and specialty goods.

Upscale dining options range from locally-owned bistros to nationally recognized restaurants, making it a culinary destination in its own right. Throughout the year, lively events such as the Stars & Stripes celebration, Oktoberfest, and the beloved Art in the Square festival bring residents together and foster the strong community spirit that defines Southlake.

Architectural Styles

Southlake's homes reflect a diverse range of architectural traditions, each contributing to the city's distinctive character.

⛫

Traditional & Colonial

Defined by symmetrical facades, pitched roofs, and classic brick or stone exteriors. These stately homes often feature elegant columns, decorative shutters, and formal entryways that evoke a timeless sense of grandeur and curb appeal.

☼

Mediterranean & Tuscan

Inspired by the warmth of Southern Europe, these homes showcase stucco and stone exteriors, red clay tile roofs, and graceful arched windows. Wrought-iron accents, lush courtyards, and shaded loggias create an inviting, resort-like atmosphere.

■

Transitional & Modern Farmhouse

Blending contemporary design with rustic warmth, these homes feature open floor plans, clean architectural lines, and natural materials such as reclaimed wood and stone. Expansive windows flood interiors with natural light and connect indoor and outdoor living spaces.
