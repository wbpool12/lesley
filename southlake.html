Luxury Living in the Heart of DFW

Southlake, Texas

Welcome to Southlake

Southlake, Texas is one of the most affluent and sought-after suburbs in the entire Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. With a population of approximately 32,000 residents, this prestigious city sits along Highway 114 between Dallas and Fort Worth, offering the perfect balance of upscale suburban living and convenient metropolitan access. Southlake is perhaps best known for Southlake Town Square, a premier mixed-use development that serves as the city's vibrant downtown and features over 140 shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues spread across a beautifully designed open-air setting.

Education is a cornerstone of life in Southlake. The city is served exclusively by Carroll Independent School District (Carroll ISD), which is consistently ranked among the top school districts in the state of Texas. Carroll ISD's academic achievements, championship athletics -- including the legendary Carroll Dragons football program -- and extensive extracurricular offerings draw families from across the country. The district's commitment to excellence is reflected in its high graduation rates, impressive college placement statistics, and strong community involvement.

Beyond its schools and shopping, Southlake offers a family-friendly community defined by beautifully maintained neighborhoods, expansive parks and trail systems, upscale dining, and a strong sense of civic pride. With a median home price exceeding $1 million, Southlake represents a premier destination for those seeking luxury homes, top-tier education, and an exceptional quality of life in North Texas.

Location of Southlake, TX

Southlake is strategically located in the northeast corner of Tarrant County, bordered by Keller to the west, Colleyville to the east, Grapevine to the southeast, and Trophy Club to the north. The city sits directly along State Highway 114, one of the primary corridors connecting Dallas and Fort Worth, and is just minutes from DFW International Airport. This central location makes Southlake ideal for professionals who commute to either downtown Dallas or Fort Worth, as well as frequent travelers who value proximity to a major international airport. State Highway 1709 (Southlake Boulevard) runs east-west through the heart of the city, providing easy access to neighboring communities and retail destinations.

Living in Southlake: Pros & Cons

5 Reasons You'll Love It

  • Top-Rated Schools (Carroll ISD)

    Carroll Independent School District is consistently recognized as one of the best in Texas, offering exceptional academics, state championship athletics, and a wide range of extracurricular programs that prepare students for success at the highest levels.

  • Southlake Town Square

    The crown jewel of the city, Southlake Town Square is a premier open-air shopping, dining, and entertainment destination with over 140 retailers and restaurants. It serves as the community's gathering place for festivals, concerts, and seasonal events throughout the year.

  • Beautiful Homes

    Southlake is home to some of the most stunning residential properties in the DFW metroplex. From grand estates on sprawling lots to meticulously designed custom builds, the city's neighborhoods showcase architectural excellence and impeccable landscaping at every turn.

  • Safe Community

    Southlake consistently ranks among the safest cities in Texas. The Southlake Department of Public Safety maintains a strong presence in the community, and the city's low crime rates provide peace of mind for families and residents alike.

  • DFW Airport Access

    Located just minutes from DFW International Airport, Southlake is ideal for frequent travelers and business professionals. Quick access to one of the nation's largest airports makes domestic and international travel remarkably convenient.

5 Reasons You May Not

  • High Cost of Living

    With a median home price exceeding $1 million, Southlake is one of the most expensive communities in the DFW area. The cost of living extends beyond housing to dining, shopping, and general day-to-day expenses, which tend to be higher than surrounding suburbs.

  • Traffic on 114/1709

    Highway 114 and Southlake Boulevard (FM 1709) can experience significant congestion, particularly during rush hours and around Southlake Town Square. As the area continues to grow, traffic has become a notable concern for daily commuters.

  • Limited Nightlife

    While Southlake Town Square offers excellent dining and entertainment, the city is primarily a residential community. Those seeking a vibrant nightlife scene with late-night bars, clubs, and live music venues may find the options limited compared to Dallas or Fort Worth.

  • HOA Restrictions

    Many Southlake neighborhoods are governed by homeowners associations with detailed guidelines regarding home exteriors, landscaping, fencing, and property modifications. While these rules help maintain property values, some residents may find the restrictions limiting.

  • Property Taxes

    Texas has no state income tax, but property taxes in Southlake can be substantial given the high assessed values of homes in the area. Combined city, county, and school district tax rates mean annual property tax bills can be significant for homeowners.

Home Styles In Southlake, TX

Southlake's residential landscape is defined by high-end custom homes, manicured streetscapes, and a diverse mix of architectural styles. Whether you are drawn to timeless traditional designs, sleek modern builds, or the increasingly popular transitional aesthetic, Southlake offers an impressive selection of luxury homes to suit every taste. Here are the three most common home styles you will find throughout the city.

Traditional

Traditional homes are the most prevalent style in Southlake, characterized by brick or stone exteriors, steep rooflines, symmetrical facades, and elegant entry features. These homes often include formal living and dining rooms, spacious kitchens with granite or marble countertops, and well-appointed master suites. Many traditional Southlake homes sit on half-acre lots or larger, with lush landscaping, circular driveways, and expansive backyards with pools and outdoor living areas. Neighborhoods like Shady Oaks, Estes Park, and Southridge Lakes showcase some of the finest traditional homes in the city.

Modern / Contemporary

Modern and contemporary homes are a growing trend in Southlake, appealing to buyers who prefer clean lines, open floor plans, and an abundance of natural light. These homes typically feature flat or low-pitched rooflines, floor-to-ceiling windows, minimalist design elements, and a seamless flow between indoor and outdoor living spaces. High-end finishes such as polished concrete, custom cabinetry, and smart home technology are standard in Southlake's modern builds. Newer developments and custom lots throughout the city are increasingly featuring this architectural style.

Transitional

Transitional homes blend the warmth and elegance of traditional architecture with the clean, open feel of contemporary design. This style has become increasingly popular in Southlake, offering the best of both worlds. Expect to see mixed material exteriors combining stone, stucco, and wood, along with open-concept interiors, large kitchen islands, and spa-inspired bathrooms. Transitional homes in Southlake often feature neutral color palettes, high ceilings, and thoughtful design details that create a sophisticated yet inviting atmosphere. This style appeals to families who want a timeless look with modern functionality.

Schools in Southlake, TX

Carroll Independent School District

Carroll ISD serves the entire city of Southlake and is widely regarded as one of the premier school districts in Texas. The district encompasses seven campuses: four elementary schools (Carroll, Durham, Johnson, and Old Union), two intermediate schools (Dawson and Eubanks), Carroll Middle School, and Carroll Senior High School. Carroll ISD consistently earns top ratings from the Texas Education Agency and is recognized nationally for academic performance.

Carroll Senior High School is particularly notable for its rigorous academic programs, including a wide selection of Advanced Placement (AP) and honors courses. The school boasts impressive college acceptance rates, with graduates regularly attending Ivy League universities and other top-tier institutions. Beyond academics, the Carroll Dragons athletic program is legendary in Texas high school sports. The football team has won multiple state championships, and the district fields competitive teams across virtually every sport.

The district also invests heavily in fine arts, technology, and career readiness programs. From award-winning theater productions and band performances to cutting-edge STEM initiatives, Carroll ISD provides students with a well-rounded education that prepares them for success in college and beyond. The strong parent involvement and community support further distinguish Carroll ISD as an exceptional place for students to learn and grow.

Places of Interest in Southlake

Southlake Town Square is the heart and soul of the city. This award-winning, open-air mixed-use development spans over 130 acres and features more than 140 shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues. From national retailers and local boutiques to fine dining and casual eateries, Town Square offers something for everyone. The central plaza hosts community events year-round, including the Stars & Stripes celebration on the Fourth of July, Oktoberfest, the Holiday Magic parade, and live music performances. It is truly the gathering place that defines the Southlake experience.

Bob Jones Park is a 188-acre nature preserve located along the shores of Lake Grapevine. The park features over three miles of hiking and mountain biking trails, picnic areas, a fishing pier, and abundant wildlife. It is one of the largest and most beautiful natural areas in the DFW metroplex and provides a peaceful retreat for outdoor enthusiasts. The park is also a popular destination for bird watching, photography, and simply enjoying the natural beauty of North Texas.

Bicentennial Park is a community favorite featuring sports fields, a covered pavilion, playground equipment, and walking trails. The park hosts youth sports leagues, family gatherings, and community events throughout the year. Its central location and well-maintained facilities make it one of the most popular parks in Southlake for families with children of all ages.

The Marq Southlake is a state-of-the-art community recreation center operated by the City of Southlake. The facility includes an indoor swimming pool, fitness center, gymnasium, group fitness studios, and meeting rooms. The Marq offers a wide range of programs for all ages, from youth swim lessons and sports camps to adult fitness classes and senior activities. It serves as a hub for health, wellness, and community engagement in Southlake.

Gateway Church is one of the largest and most well-known megachurches in the United States, with its main campus located in Southlake. Founded in 2000, Gateway has grown to serve tens of thousands of members across multiple campuses throughout the DFW area. The Southlake campus features a modern worship center, extensive children's and youth ministry facilities, and hosts a variety of community outreach programs. Gateway Church plays a significant role in the fabric of the Southlake community.
